🌍 Free Public Remote Sensing Datasets

Our models and validation benchmarks leverage internationally established, open-access remote sensing datasets covering multi-spectral optical and Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery.

Optical + SAR Multimodal

BigEarthNet

Sentinel-1 & Sentinel-2 Benchmark

Massive multi-modal benchmark dataset containing 590,326 pairs of Sentinel-1 dual-polarization SAR and Sentinel-2 12-band multi-spectral optical image tiles across 10 European nations.

Total Pairs: 590,326 Sentinel Image Tiles
Resolution: 10m, 20m, 60m GSD
Primary Tasks: Optical-SAR Multimodal Fusion, Land-Cover
bigearth.net
Visual Question Answering

RSVQA

Remote Sensing Visual Question Answering

Pioneering benchmark for visual question answering over satellite imagery, featuring query-answer pairs evaluating object presence, counts, comparison, and spatial relationships.

Tiers: Low-Res (Sentinel-2, 10m) & High-Res (0.15m)
QA Pairs: 1,000,000+ Question-Answer Pairs
Tasks: Object Count, Area Comparison, Rural vs Urban
rsvqa.sylvainlobry.com
Visual Grounding Benchmark

VRSBench

Visual Remote Sensing Grounding Benchmark

Comprehensive multi-task visual remote sensing dataset providing fine-grained text descriptions, visual grounding bounding boxes, and object-level localization for satellite queries.

Size: 29,614 High-Resolution Optical Images
Annotations: 120,000+ Grounding Bounding Boxes
Tasks: Dense Captioning, Visual Grounding
github.com/lx709/VRSBench
Change Detection VQA

CDVQA

Change Detection Visual Question Answering

Dedicated bi-temporal remote sensing dataset focused on questions regarding land-use changes, urban development, disaster damage, and temporal variations between satellite image pairs.

Format: Bi-Temporal Image Pairs (T1 vs T2)
Focus: Urban Sprawl, Deforestation, Flood Inundation
Tasks: Bi-Temporal Change Detection & QA
github.com/YZHJessica/CDVQA
